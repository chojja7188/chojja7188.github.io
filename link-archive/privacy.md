# Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-13

**Summary.** Without sign-in, your links stay only on your device and we receive nothing. If you sign in with Google, your account info and saved links are stored on our server (Supabase) to sync across devices. We do not sell your data or use analytics/tracking tools.

**What we collect.** (a) On Google sign-in: email, name and profile photo (if provided by Google), Google account ID. (b) When syncing: link URLs, descriptions, thumbnails (emoji or image URL), category names, favorite flags, created/updated/last-opened times. (c) Server logs (IP address, timestamps) handled by our hosting provider for security. The app requests no device permissions (location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone).

**Processed only on your device (never sent to us).** *Clipboard* — on Android, when you open the Add Link screen the app checks the clipboard once and shows a "Paste" button only if it holds a link; on iOS it reads the clipboard only when you tap Apple's Paste button. The content is checked on the device and is not stored or sent. *Shared links* — a link shared from another app only fills in the Add Link screen; it is saved only when you tap Save. *Title & thumbnail auto-fill* — when you enter a new link, the app connects directly to that website to read its page title and preview image. This does not go through our server or any other company, but the website can see connection details (such as your IP address) just as when you visit it in a browser. *Backup files* — created only when you tap "Create Backup File"; you choose where to save it (e.g., Google Drive, email). The app does not send it anywhere; loading reads only the file you pick. *Language setting* — stored only on your device.

**Retention.** Data is deleted from the server immediately when you delete your account (Settings › Delete account). Signing out removes data from that device.

**Processors / international transfer.** Supabase, Inc. (Japan — AWS Tokyo region, ap-northeast-1) — storage and authentication. Google LLC (USA) — Google sign-in. Data is sent over HTTPS when you sign in or sync.

**Security.** All traffic uses HTTPS; row-level security limits access to your own data; app data is excluded from Android auto-backup so it is not copied to Google's cloud backup.

**Your rights.** View and edit in the app; delete your account in Settings; other requests by email.

**Children.** Not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We delete such data when we learn of it.

**Contact.** Link Archive developer · linkarchive.app@gmail.com

**Changes.** We will announce changes in the app or on this page before they take effect.
