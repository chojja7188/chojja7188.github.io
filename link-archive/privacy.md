# Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-13

**Summary.** Without sign-in, your links stay only on your device and we receive none of your content; only error reports are sent if the app crashes or hits an error. If you sign in with Google, your account info and saved links are stored on our server (Supabase) to sync across devices. We do not sell your data or use advertising, behavioral analytics or tracking tools.

**What we collect.** (a) On Google sign-in: email, name and profile photo (if provided by Google), Google account ID. (b) When syncing: link URLs, descriptions, thumbnails (emoji or image URL), category names, favorite flags, created/updated/last-opened times. (c) Server logs (IP address, timestamps) handled by our hosting provider for security, kept up to 24 hours. (d) Error reports, sent automatically when the app crashes or hits an error: the error and where it happened, device model, OS and app version, a random per-install identifier, and recent in-app actions (such as screen changes). They never include your saved links, email or IP address (the error-reporting provider may infer an approximate country/city from the connection). Kept up to 90 days, used only to find and fix bugs. The app requests no device permissions (location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone), and uses no cookies, advertising IDs or other automatic tracking technologies. Sign-in data is processed with your consent; server logs and error reports are processed as needed to run the service securely.

**Processed only on your device (never sent to us).** *Clipboard* — on Android, when you open the Add Link screen the app checks the clipboard once and shows a "Paste" button only if it holds a link; on iOS it reads the clipboard only when you tap Apple's Paste button. The content is checked on the device and is not stored or sent. *Shared links* — a link shared from another app only fills in the Add Link screen; it is saved only when you tap Save. *Title & thumbnail auto-fill* — when you enter a new link, the app connects directly to that website to read its page title and preview image. This does not go through our server or any other company, but the website can see connection details (such as your IP address) just as when you visit it in a browser. Links with an image-URL thumbnail also load that image directly from its website whenever the list shows it. *Backup files* — created only when you tap "Create Backup File"; you choose where to save it (e.g., Google Drive, email). The app does not send it anywhere; loading reads only the file you pick. *Language setting* — stored only on your device.

**Retention.** Account and synced data are deleted from the server immediately when you delete your account (Account & Sync › Delete Account). When you delete an individual link, its description, thumbnail, and category associations are cleared after sync. A deletion record, including the link ID, up to the first 100 characters of the URL, and timestamps, remains until you delete your account. Signing out removes data from that device. Deleted data is erased so it cannot be recovered.

**Processors / international transfer.** Supabase, Inc. (Japan — AWS Tokyo region, ap-northeast-1) — storage and authentication. Google LLC (USA) — Google sign-in. Functional Software, Inc. / Sentry (USA) — error reports. Data is sent over HTTPS when you sign in or sync.

**Security.** All traffic uses HTTPS; row-level security limits access to your own data; app data is excluded from Android auto-backup so it is not copied to Google's cloud backup.

**Your rights.** View and edit in the app; delete your account in Account & Sync; other requests by email.

**Children.** Not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We delete such data when we learn of it.

**Contact.** Link Archive developer · linkarchive.app@gmail.com

**Changes.** We will announce changes in the app or on this page before they take effect.
